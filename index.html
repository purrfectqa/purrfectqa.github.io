Sample report

A finding your team can reproduce.

A timeout can leave a successful payment looking like a failure. The report connects the observed states to the decision your team needs to make.

From a fictional release risk brief for a subscription checkout.

PAY-01 / Release risk brief High priority

Checkout offers a retry after payment approval.

Observed
The provider approves the payment. Until the backend catches up, checkout displays “Payment timed out. Try again.”
Risk
A customer may start a second payment attempt while the first one is unresolved in the app.
Reproduction steps and recommendation
  1. In the test environment, start a subscription payment.
  2. Have the provider approve it while delaying delivery of the confirmation event until after checkout times out.
  3. Compare the provider status, backend status and checkout message before the delayed event arrives.
Expected behavior
Show a pending state while the payment outcome is being reconciled. Recheck the existing attempt before letting the customer start another.
Coverage limit
A second charge was outside this example’s test scope.
